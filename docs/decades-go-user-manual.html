Getting Started

Decades GO is the contracting and facilities management platform for Decades Construction & Facilities Group. It manages customers, contracts, onboarding, locations, vendors, projects, and compliance — all in one place.

How To: Log In

Sign-in screen — click Sign In, then authenticate with Windows Hello

Log In (step by step)

Open your browser
Go to your portal URL (bookmarked or provided by your admin).
Click "Sign in"
You'll see a dark blue Sign in button in the center of the page.
Authenticate with Windows Hello
Your computer will prompt you for a fingerprint, face scan, or PIN. Complete it.
You're in
The Sales Dashboard loads automatically. Your name appears at the bottom-left of the sidebar.
Your session stays active across browser tabs. Bookmark your portal URL for quick access.

How To: Navigate the System

Left sidebar with CREATE shortcuts, nav groups, and user footer

Find your way around

CREATE shortcuts (top of sidebar)
Six quick-create buttons: Proposal, Vendor Agreement, Work Order, Amendment, RFP Package, Project. Click any one to jump straight into that wizard.
Nav groups (middle of sidebar)
Five collapsible groups: Sales, Facilities, Projects, Management, Admin. Click the arrow next to a group name to expand it and see the screens inside.
User Manual link
Below the CREATE shortcuts, click User Manual to open this guide inside the app.
Your user footer (bottom of sidebar)
Shows your name and a sign-out button. This confirms who you're logged in as.

Client Service Agreements (MSAs)

Client Service Agreements — agreement name, customer, family badge, current amendment, trades, and budget

Go to Locations
Click Facilities in the sidebar, then Locations.
Click "+ New Location"
Blue button, top-right corner.
Enter the location name
Use the naming convention: ACP-TownName-Number (e.g., ACP-Burlington-16).
Pick the customer
Select the customer this location belongs to from the dropdown.
Set the type
Choose: Admin, Apartment, Day Program, Group Home, School, etc.
Enter the address
Full street address, city, state, zip.
Save
Click Save. The location appears in the list and is available for contracts.
After creating a location, click into it to fill out the full profile: Facility Profile, Building Systems, Compliance Documents, and Appliances tabs.

Go to Onboarding

Blue button, top-right corner.

How To: Create a New Work Order

Contract Wizard — shows your saved drafts and the "+ Start New Contract" button
Open the Contract Wizard
Click Work Order in the CREATE shortcuts on the left sidebar.
Click "+ Start New Contract"
Gold button below any saved drafts. (If you have drafts, you'll see them listed with Resume/Delete buttons.)
Step 1 — Pick the Customer
Search by name and click to select. The system detects the customer family (Decades vs TPA) and picks the right templates automatically.
Step 2 — Document Type & Details
Choose Work Order. Select the location from the dropdown, pick the vendor, and set start/end dates.
Step 3 — Contractor & Signer
Vendor details auto-fill from the vendor record. Enter the signer name and title, and the owner contact info.
Step 4 — Exhibit A Line Items
Click + Add Line to add each line item. For each line: pick a cost code, enter a description, and type the amount. The total calculates automatically at the bottom.
Step 5 — Review & Generate
Review all details. The compliance checklist shows which checks pass or fail (does NOT block you). Click Generate to create the document. It goes into the Send Queue for approval.
Your draft saves automatically at every step. You can close the browser and come back later — click Resume on the drafts screen to pick up where you left off.
You do NOT assign a WO number. Send Queue staff assigns the WO number after reviewing your submission.

How To: Create an Amendment

Open the Contract Wizard
Click Amendment in the CREATE shortcuts on the left sidebar.
Click "+ Start New Contract"
Gold button at the bottom of the drafts list.
Step 1 — Pick the Customer
Search and select the customer.
Step 2 — Choose "Amendment" and pick the parent Work Order
Select Amendment as the document type. Then select the parent Work Order you are amending from the dropdown. This is required — every amendment must reference an existing WO.
Steps 3-5 — Same as Work Order
Fill in contractor/signer (Step 3), Exhibit A lines (Step 4), review and generate (Step 5). The system uses the amendment template automatically.

How To: Create a Vendor Agreement

Open the Contract Wizard
Click Vendor Agreement in the CREATE shortcuts on the left sidebar.
Click "+ Start New Contract"
Gold button at the bottom.
Step 1 — Pick the Customer
Search and select.
Step 2 — Choose "Vendor MSA"
Select Vendor MSA as the document type. Pick the vendor. No parent WO is needed.
Steps 3-5 — Complete the wizard
Fill in contractor/signer details, review, and generate. The Vendor MSA template is selected automatically.

How To: Create a Proposal

Proposal Wizard — drafts list and "+ Start New Proposal" button
Open the Proposal Wizard
Click Proposal in the CREATE shortcuts on the left sidebar.
Click "+ Start New Proposal"
Gold button below any saved drafts.
Step 1 — Service Package
Choose: Package A (Essential), Package B (Extended), or Package C (Premium). This determines which Exhibit A template is used.
Step 2 — Customer & Vendor
Search for an existing customer or create a new one. Enter MSA details, signer info, and billing address.
Step 3 — Pricing & Locations
Set the monthly membership rate and onboarding fee. Choose uniform or type-based pricing. Add locations.
Step 4 — Review & Generate
Review everything. Click Generate Proposal. This creates the MSA record, rate records, and triggers document generation.

How To: Resume a Saved Draft

Open the wizard for your document type
Click Work Order, Amendment, Vendor Agreement, or Proposal in the CREATE shortcuts.
Find your draft
Your saved drafts appear at the top of the wizard screen. Each shows the customer name, document type, location, which step you were on, and when it was last saved.
Click "Resume"
The wizard opens to the exact step where you left off. All your data is preserved.
To delete a draft you don't need
Click the red Delete button next to the draft. It's removed permanently.

How To: Approve a Document

Go to Send Queue
Click Management in the sidebar, then Send Queue.
Find the document
Look in the Pending Review tab. Documents show the customer, contract type, and who submitted it.
Review it
Click the document row to expand it. Check the details: customer, location, vendor, amounts, dates.
Take action
Approve — moves it to the Approved tab, ready for sending.
Request Info — sends it back with a note asking for clarification.
Return — sends it back to the creator to fix and resubmit.
Delete — removes the document (soft delete).
Assign WO Number (if needed)
If the document appears in the Needs WO Number tab, enter the WO number and click Save. This is done by Send Queue staff, not the contract creator.
WO numbers are assigned by Send Queue staff, not by the person who created the contract.
