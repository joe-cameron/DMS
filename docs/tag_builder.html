Click any value to copy. Switch between Word and Flow modes.

Mode: Word — Content Controls Flow — Populate Expressions
Word Content Controls: 1. Open template in desktop Word 2. Developer > Plain Text Content Control 3. Click control > Developer > Properties 4. Click a tag below to copy 5. Paste into Title field 6. Paste into Tag field 7. OK 8. Repeat 9. Save as .docx 10. Upload to SharePoint DCFG_Templates
Flow Populate Fields: 1. Open flow designer > Switch_Template > your template case 2. Click Populate action 3. Check Show all under Advanced parameters 4. Check each field's checkbox 5. Click in the field > switch to Expression tab 6. Click the expression below to copy 7. Paste into the expression box 8. Click OK 9. Repeat 10. Save flow
