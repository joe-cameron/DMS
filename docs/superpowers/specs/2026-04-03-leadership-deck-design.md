# DCFG Leadership Deck — Design Spec

**Date:** 2026-04-03
**Format:** PowerPoint, 3 slides, ~5 minutes talk time
**Audience:** Decades leadership team
**Narrative:** Technology + planning enables 10X revenue scale ($10M to $100M) without proportional headcount growth. Two anchor products — Contracts and Onboarding — tell the compound story. Everything else is embellishment that fades into the background.

---

## Animation Technique

All content slides use **gentle layered reveal animation**:
- Each beat gently fades in on spacebar press
- Previous beat gently fades to ~30% opacity / muted color
- Current beat is full brightness
- Transitions are soft and unhurried — nothing snaps or pops
- Final state: anchor content visible, embellishments muted in background

## Visual Style

- Clean, minimal text per beat
- Speaker narrates, slides punctuate
- No screenshots — this is a story deck, not a demo
- Muted background elements hint at depth without demanding attention

---

## Slide 1 — ROI (~30 seconds)

**Headline:** ROI

Sub-beats (static or quick reveal):
- Built by one person + AI in 2 months
- $600-$1,200/yr to operate (pay-as-you-go Microsoft licensing)
- Enables $10M → $100M with +2-3 staff

**Tone:** Let the ROI speak for itself. No AI philosophy, no buzzwords. This is what it cost, this is what it does.

---

## Slide 2 — Contracts (anchor, ~2 minutes)

Layered reveal sequence:

1. **Contract creation + approval workflow** — the core. Replaces Word docs and email chains with structured workflow and approval gates.
2. **Customer database** — emerged from contracts. You didn't buy a CRM — you built a contracting system and a CRM fell out of it.
3. **Document automation** — proposals, contracts, exhibits. Generated, not typed. DocGen pipeline produces Word documents from templates, converts to PDF, merges, sends.
4. **Background muted: platform + AI harness** — the engine underneath. $1,200/yr infrastructure, AI agents continuously improving the system.

---

## Slide 3 — Onboarding (anchor, ~2 minutes)

Layered reveal sequence:

1. **Customer onboarding process** — the core. Replaces manual intake with guided, digital property data collection.
2. **Locations + scheduling** — operational backbone emerged from onboarding new properties.
3. **Process task management** — phased steps, due dates, progress tracking, evidence capture.
4. **Field ops** (embellishment) — jumping-off point for remote inspectors.
5. **Delegation** (embellishment) — customer's own people enter the data. Many hands make light work when you're not paying the hands.
6. **Background muted: AI knowledge layer** — the system knows itself. Training assistance, information retrieval, troubleshooting. Every click logged, Nora monitors and surfaces issues.

---

## Story Arc

The compound story: each system wasn't just solving one problem, it was laying foundation for the next.

- Slide 1 hooks them with the economics
- Slide 2 shows Contracts built a CRM and doc automation as side effects
- Slide 3 shows Onboarding built ops management and delegated work to customers
- The muted background on both anchors whispers: there's an AI engine and a $25K platform underneath all of this

**The audience should leave thinking:** "One person built this with AI in two months, it costs $25K/yr to run, and it's why we can 10X without 10X staff."
